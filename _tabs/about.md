**Paths Not Taken** is an archive of working notes on counterfactuals, irreversibility, curiosity and
abstraction in learning systems built from people and machines.

The essays, working papers and synthetic panel discussions here were written with an AI assistant (Claude,
by Anthropic) during November 2026. The panels use synthetic voices. Read them as speculative syntheses,
not peer-reviewed work. Each post links the full PDF, and some include an audio panel discussion.

Some posts discuss an art project: LED creatures that react to music. It's a research playground. No code,
hardware designs or repositories are offered, and there are no comments. This site is a record, not a project.
