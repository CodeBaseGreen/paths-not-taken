A reading compendium (42 pages) collecting the thirty papers that underpin
[Paths Not Taken]({{ '/posts/paths-not-taken/' | relative_url }}) and
[Copies, Lineages, and Noise]({{ '/posts/copies-lineages-and-noise/' | relative_url }}).

The papers are grouped in seven parts that follow the argument: the language of causation, irreversibility,
substrates, representations, mathematics, curiosity, and development. Each part opens with a short orientation.

Each paper gets about a page in three parts:

- **The abstract in brief:** a plain-language summary of what the paper says, with a link to the original.
- **Recapitulation:** the main idea restated in the terms of the discussion, and why it matters there.
- **Connections:** links to the sections of the essays it supports, and to other papers in the collection.

There's a thread map after the contents and an index of concepts at the end.

**If you read only five:** Pearl on the ladder of causation; Dwork and colleagues on why reusing a test set
overfits; Kanerva on the algebra of hypervectors; Schmidhuber on compression progress; and Zador on why a compact
genome forces structure.
